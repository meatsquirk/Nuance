## Transparency notes (do not drop any row below A)

1. **AC-3 is the thinnest on G1** — it reads `harness.colorScheme` with no explicit `MaterialApp`/`Scaffold` render assertion (AC-1 carries those). The "theme installed" precondition is nonetheless met transitively: the `installedTheme` getter dereferences `find.byType(MaterialApp).first` and `theme!`, so the read fails unless a themed app assembled. Combined with exact-value + `isNot(stock)`, discrimination is airtight. A belt-and-suspenders `find.byType(MaterialApp)` would strengthen it but is not required for correctness or discrimination; not gate-blocking.
2. **AC-2's Given names "dividers"** but asserts only that `Text` renders. Immaterial: the neutrals (including `outline`/`outlineVariant`) are read at the theme-role grain — the single source — not off a rendered `Divider`, so a divider-presence assertion would add nothing to discrimination.
3. **AC-6 asserts a static theme source, not a mounted widget.** This is the deliberate and faithful choice for a THEME-4-owned family AC under D-7 (the source is the authority; mounting a `MonoLabel` would couple the pass to COMPONENTS wiring that THEME-4 does not own). It fully discriminates the family-in-theme claim at design time. The separate claim "a rendered micro-label/readout consumes this source" is AC-9's (mounted `MonoLabel`) and the APPLY phases', not AC-6's — hence no augmentation is scheduled or owed for AC-6.
4. **AC-4 re-reads the AC-1/2/3 tokens** as its three preconditions. This is the spec's own Given ("the warm-paper theme is installed") and is required by G2, not a G6 violation — it establishes the configured state the AA floor is measured against; the graded outcome is solely the `>= 4.5` floor.
