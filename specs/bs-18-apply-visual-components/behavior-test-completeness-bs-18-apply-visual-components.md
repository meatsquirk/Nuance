(b) `AppTopBar` renders its `leadingKey` only when `onBack`/`onClose` is non-null, and the harness pumps every deep-link screen **as a root** (`pumpScreen` boots a fresh app rooted at that screen), so no back can be reliably asserted on this roster. (c) Neither of AC-2's **catalogue Rejects** ("ad-hoc header without the toggle"; "hardcoded title style") concerns the affordance — both are fully caught. The screens that genuinely need a back/close (Display settings, onboarding) are the CFG screens graded under **AC-10 (ITEST-3)**, not AC-2. If the author wishes to belt-and-suspender it, a cheap addition is `expect(find.byKey(AppTopBar.leadingKey), findsNothing)` on each root surface (asserting the *absence* the roots need) — but this is a strengthening, not a correctness gap, so AC-2 grades **straight A**. Marked ◑ on G4 for the omission; not gate-blocking.
2. **AC-1 is green at baseline but fully discriminates at design time.** CHROME-2 already shipped the conformant nav (4 destinations, filled-vs-outline active marker, theme-token fill/hairline), so all AC-1 assertions pass at the red-baseline run. CHROME-3 (AC-1's owner) un-pends it and keeps it green; no augmentation is pre-seeded or owed for AC-1.
3. **AC-3 and AC-5 are A-core by the pre-seeded augmentation table, not plain B.** Both author the *present-side* of the swap and catch their catalogue's **named** Rejects (AC-3: torch/import/shutter ad-hoc; AC-5: bare-Column region / IconButton speak).
