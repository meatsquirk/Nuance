# bs-08 Display, simulation and spoken output — behaviour-test completeness

Independent adversarial grade of the bs-08 acceptance integration tests as they stand (ITEST-1 artifacts + ITEST-2 per-AC tests AC-1..AC-7). Grader did not write the tests; no test was modified. Pending tests are graded on design regardless of skip state. Surfaces checked: `integration_test/display_test.dart`, `display_harness.dart`, `bs08/pending.dart`, `fakes/*`, and the product render path (`color_chip.dart`, `color_simulation.dart`, `settings_screen.dart`, `grayscale_filter.dart`, `display_controller.dart`/`display_settings.dart`/`display_read_endpoint.dart`, `readout/value_region.dart`, `widgets/value_reading.dart`, `spoken_output_panel.dart`). Confirmed `ColorChip` is the only circular `BoxDecoration` colour swatch on the Readout (the value-region grayscale preview is a rectangle; the Capture eyedropper circle is on another screen), so `_displaySwatches` reads exactly the simulation chokepoint and is empty at baseline.

## Verdict

ITEST-2: **6 A + 1 B pending DISPLAY-8**. Below A: **AC-7 — B pending DISPLAY-8** (G5: green-at-baseline and limited; it asserts value+label are present but, with no swatch rendered until DISPLAY-4, cannot yet reject a swatch-without-text simulated reading — the discriminating "swatch coexists with value+label" step is the DISPLAY-8 augmentation). AC-1 and AC-2..AC-6 fully discriminate their own ACs' steps against the right surfaces (live `DisplayReadEndpoint`, the circular `ColorChip` `_displaySwatches` graded via the independent `referenceProjectDichromat`→shared `toSRGB`, and the root `ColorFiltered` ancestor of `MaterialApp`).
